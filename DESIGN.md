## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the stillness, deep lineage, and living horticultural wealth of an equatorial estate in coastal Karnataka. Drawing inspiration from quiet luxury sanctuaries and rare botanical monographs, the visual language balances monastic restraint with scientific precision. 

The emotional resonance is meditative, archival, and timeless. The interface recedes entirely, serving as an exhibition plane for documentary photography, indigenous agronomy records, and estate provenance. Rather than treating nature as decorative, the design treats the landscape as an enduring archive: measured, patient, and unhurried.

Key visual tenets:
- **Architectural Stillness**: Compositions prioritize deliberate negative space, razor-sharp alignment, and structural gravitas over dynamic decoration.
- **Archival Precision**: High-contrast, literary serifs meet functional modern sans-serifs, evoking botanical specimen catalogs, herbarium registries, and estate ledgers.
- **Material Restraint**: Surfaces exist as deep peat, basalt, and shadowed canopy tones. There are no luminous neon highlights, floating drop shadows, or synthetic glass effects.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in dark, soil-rich mineral tones, sun-bleached parchment, and understated botanical brass. The environment is deliberately low-luminance, guiding focus toward deliberate imagery and editorial type.

### Palette Implementation
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0B0A`)**: A deep, peat-tinted black canvas serving as the default ground.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#111311`)**: Deepest foliage shadow; used for panels, secondary sections, and structural cards.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#161916`)**: Basalt moss; used for interactive states, dropdown surfaces, and active card segments.
- **Sub-surface / Structural Inset (`#1F221E`)**: A dense, olive-charcoal used for subtle structural emphasis, contrast containers, and selected indicator plates.
- **Primary Text (`#EAE4D8`)**: Warm parchment chalk; delivers soft legibility against dark grounds without the eye fatigue of pure white.
- **Secondary Text (`#A8A49B`)**: Warm weathered stone; calibrated for body paragraphs, documentary narratives, and extended captions.
- **Muted Metadata (`#706D66`)**: Dried riverbed silt; reserved for timestamps, registration numbers, and inactive states.
- **Accent Antique Gold (`#B99868`)**: A muted brass alloy; applied with strict restraint for hairline rules, botanical taxon labels, coordinates, and active signifiers. Never used for massive flooded fills.
- **Hairline Border (`#34312B`)**: An earthen hairline boundary with zero glow, providing geometric precision without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical editorial serifs with low-contrast, functional grotesque sans-serifs:

- **EB Garamond** serves as the poetic voice. Headings are set predominantly in sentence case to foster an understated, literary cadence. Display sizes favor tighter, contemplative line-heights and slight negative tracking. Botanical nomenclature and historical excerpts use Garamond's elegant italic cuts.
- **DM Sans** acts as the archival apparatus. Its clean geometry handles long-form estate chronicles, index cards, technical coordinates, harvest yields, and operational labels.
- **Caps & Micro-Tracking**: All uppercase headings and metadata labels require wide letter-spacing (`0.18em` to `0.22em`). They must always be rendered in `label-caps-sm` or `label-caps-lg` to mirror field notebook taxonomy and heritage inventory stamping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an asymmetric editorial 12-column grid that mirrors fine art publications and exhibition monographs.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column layout with `margin-desktop` (4rem) and `gutter-desktop` (2.5rem). Elements embrace deliberate negative space: wide 7-column editorial prose offset by 3-column archival sidebars and 2 empty buffer columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with 2rem margins and 1.5rem gutters. Content reflows into a more balanced sequence; asymmetrical pairs shift to staggered full-width sections.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `margin` (1.25rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Typographic scales gracefully collapse to mobile-dedicated display sizes; side-by-side archival data converts to clean stacked ledgers.

### Spatial Rhythm
Layout breathing room relies on generous vertical cadence (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between sections. Components never crowd one another; wide dark planes give each image and specimen listing the focus of an archival exhibition.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy physical shadows, luminous drop-shadows, and skeuomorphic embossing. Depth is articulated purely through tonal stratification and planar boundary lines:

1. **Planar Layering**: Visual depth moves inward rather than floating outward. Ground (`#0B0B0A`) hosts primary structural modules in Deep Foliage (`#111311`), with active or elevated trays shifting to Basalt Moss (`#161916`).
2. **Hairline Boundaries**: Structural division relies on `1px solid #34312B`. Borders are never decorative flourishes; they are crisp, architectural joinery marking spatial transitions.
3. **Ghost Borders on Imagery**: Archival photographs and botanical specimens sit flush against hairline grid frames, occasionally inset with an internal `1px` translucent edge to separate deep photo shadows from the deep peat background.
4. **Zero Ambient Shadows**: Cards and dialogs do not float on blurred clouds. Modals and drawers slide along a singular flat plane, defined strictly by a `#34312B` outline and an opaque background.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly architectural. 

- All containers, cards, inputs, buttons, and visual frames use `roundedness: 0` (sharp 90-degree corners).
- Rounded corners diminish the solemnity of heritage records; crisp geometric perimeters evoke hand-bound folios, framed herbarium glass, and granite boundary markers.
- Rounded pills, bulbous form controls, and circular badge containers are strictly prohibited across the system.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action**: A sharp rectangular silhouette. Solid `#EAE4D8` fill with `#0B0B0A` text, or a transparent frame with a 1px `#B99868` border and `#EAE4D8` text. Typography is set in `label-caps-lg`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover state introduces a quiet background transition to `#161916` with text shifting to `#B99868`.
- **Secondary / Archival Link**: Borderless text action set in `label-caps-sm` with a persistent 1px hairline underline in `#B99868` offset by 4px. Hover state draws the underline smoothly from 40% opacity to 100%.

### Chips & Botanical Specimen Tags
- Zero border-radius. Constructed with a 1px solid `#34312B` border and a background of `#111311`. 
- Content uses `label-caps-sm` in `#B99868` or `#A8A49B`.
- Padding is compact (`0.25rem 0.625rem`). Used exclusively for classification (e.g., `ORD. RUTACEAE`, `ZONE 10B`, `PLOT 04`).

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Text Inputs**: Flat, minimalist bottom-border structure (`1px solid #34312B`) or a full box frame in `#111311` with sharp corners. Background is transparent or `#0B0B0A`. Input text is rendered in `body-md` (`#EAE4D8`).
- **Focus State**: Hairline transition to `#B99868` with no drop-shadow or outer halo. Placeholder text is set in `#706D66`.
- **Labels**: Fixed above the field in `label-caps-sm` using `#A8A49B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes**: Pure square boxes (16px × 16px), 1px solid `#34312B`. Checked state fills the box with `#1F221E` and an internal sharp antique gold `#B99868` checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Strict square outer box (16px × 16px) housing a solid 6px × 6px sharp inner square in `#B99868` when active. Circular radios are forbidden to maintain architectural cohesion.

### Archival Lists & Ledgers
- Minimalist horizontal lists divided by hairline rules (`1px solid #34312B`).
- Structured in a 3-part ledger format: Identification code / Latin name on the left (`botanical-italic`), human narrative in the center (`body-md`), and harvest metric or coordinates pinned to the right (`label-caps-sm`).

### Cards & Specimen Folios
- Background: Surface 1 (`#111311`). Border: `1px solid #34312B`. 
- Padding: `space-lg` (2rem). Cards feature no drop shadow.
- Image pairings within cards use documentary aspect ratios (4:5, 3:2, or 16:9 full-bleed crops) with high color fidelity and desaturated warm balances.
- Metadata is arranged above the visual in `label-caps-sm`, with poetic titles below in `headline-md`.

### Botanical Coordinates & Metadata Banners
- A specialized domain component: full-width horizontal strip (`1px solid #34312B` top and bottom) running across the screen.
- Displays latitude, longitude, elevation above sea level, and soil moisture indices in spaced sans-serif (`label-caps-sm`) with antique gold bullet delimiters.
